Keurig account

Sign in through Keurig in your browser. Your password stays with Keurig.

Before signing in again, stop the Keurig child bridge from the Plugins page. Start it again after saving your sign-in.

  1. Open Keurig sign-in in a new browser tab.
  2. In that tab, open browser developer tools, choose Network, enable Preserve log, then sign in.
  3. In Network, copy the complete Request URL for www.keurig.com/authCallBack containing code=.
  4. Paste that URL below and save your sign-in.

Keep this URL private. Closing settings cancels an unfinished sign-in.
